# Reference PETG Print Profile

## Printer

* Creality Ender 5 Pro
* OrcaSlicer
* Klipper

## Material

* Sunlu PETG

## Temperatures

* Nozzle: **245°C**
* Bed: **75°C**

## Nozzle

* 0.4 mm Hardened Steel

## Layer Height

* 0.20 mm

## Walls

* 4

## Infill

* 20% Gyroid

## Bed Mesh

* Klipper 9×9 mesh
* Mesh loaded before PETG prints.

## Validated Cooling

* Dual 5015 part cooling fans.
* First 5 layers: fan off.
* Full cooling from Layer 7.
* Overhang / bridge fan: 70%.
